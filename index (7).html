    Definitions, data handling and what persists

    An open task is any task whose status is not Done, Complete, Completed, Closed or Cancelled. A task is overdue when it is open and its due date is before the overdue check date. Tasks with no due date or an invalid one are never counted as overdue; they are listed under Missing information.

    Estimated hours are planned working effort, not elapsed time, and are never turned into delivery dates. Non-numeric estimates are kept exactly as written and flagged.

    Filtered figures count only tasks matching the search and filters. Each card also shows the figure for the full tracker. Missing information always covers the full tracker.

    Persistence: this static page has no server or database. Uploaded files are read in your browser, never sent anywhere, and kept in memory only. Refreshing or closing the page clears the upload and restores the demo data.

    Demo data is a snapshot of the fictional task tracker Google Sheet taken on 20 Nov 2026. It does not update live. Its proposed dates, schedule and team lead priorities are cleared whenever another tracker is loaded and are never applied to uploaded records.

    Dates in a CSV can be written as 30 Nov 2026, 2026-11-30 or 30/11/2026. Slash dates are read day first.

    Nothing on this page is simulated. Validation, loading, filtering and downloads all really happen in your browser. Features that need a server, such as live Google Sheet data, shared decision logging and keeping an upload after a refresh, are not included.
